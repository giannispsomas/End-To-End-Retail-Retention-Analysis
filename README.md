# Online Retail Analysis Dashboard

Analysis of ~1M UK online retail transactions (2009–2011) to identify repeat customers, uncover the behavioral variables driving repeat purchases, and surface top-selling products — translated into actionable marketing and operations recommendations via SQL, Python, and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Project Overview

**Business Problem:** How can the company leverage its transaction data to identify repeat customers, uncover the variables driving repeat purchasing behavior, and use these insights—alongside top-selling product data—to optimize marketing and operations strategy?

**Project Objective:** Analyze historical transaction data to identify repeat customers and the variables associated with repeat purchasing behavior, determine top-selling products, and translate these findings into actionable recommendations for marketing and operations strategy.

## Business Questions

- Which are the highest selling products (by revenue and by quantity)?
- What is the price of these products?
- Which countries have the most repeat customers?
- How many of these top-selling products appear in the orders of repeat customers?
- What is the average time gap between a customer's first and second purchase?
- What proportion of total revenue comes from repeat customers vs. one-time customers?

## KPIs

- Repeat Purchase Rate — % of customers with more than one order
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) — total revenue per customer over the observed period
- Average Order Value (AOV) — total revenue / number of orders
- Revenue Share by Top Products — % of total revenue generated by top N products
- Repeat Customer Revenue Share — % of total revenue from repeat vs. one-time customers
- Time Between First and Second Purchase — average days to repeat
- Repeat Customers by Country — count/% of repeat customers per country

## Dataset

- **Source:** Online Retail II (UCI Machine Learning Repository)
- **Sheets:** Two sheets — "Year 2009-2010" and "Year 2010-2011" (combined grain: one row per product line item within an invoice)
- **Size:** 43.5 MB
- **Rows:** 1,067,371 (combined)
- **Columns:** Invoice, StockCode, Description, Quantity, InvoiceDate, Price, Customer ID, Country

> Note: `online_retail_II.xlsx` is excluded from version control via `.gitignore` due to its size. Download it from the [UCI Machine Learning Repository](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/502/online+retail+ii) and place it in the project root before running any scripts.

4. **Data Connectivity mode:** Import (recommended given dataset size ~43.5 MB — DirectQuery is unnecessary here)
5. **Authentication:** Choose Windows Authentication (default)

## Assumptions

- Transactions with an Invoice number beginning with "C" are treated as cancellations/returns and excluded from revenue and repeat-purchase calculations unless otherwise noted.
- Rows with missing Customer ID are excluded from customer-level analysis (repeat rate, CLV) but retained for product-level analysis.
- "Repeat customer" is defined as a Customer ID associated with 2 or more distinct, non-cancelled Invoice numbers.
- Price is treated as unit selling price in GBP; no cost-of-goods data is available, so profitability/margin is out of scope unless a cost dataset is added later.

## Definition of Done

This project is considered complete when all defined business questions have been answered using SQL and/or Python, the KPIs are calculated and validated against the raw dataset, the dashboard displays all core KPIs with functioning filters (by country, date range, and customer segment), and a written summary translates the findings into at least three actionable recommendations for marketing and operations.
